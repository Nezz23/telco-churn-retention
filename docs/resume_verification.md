## Revenue at risk is not retention profit

They answer different questions, and the original claim conflated them.

- **Revenue at risk** = Σ P(churn) × horizon × monthly bill. It measures what churn would cost if nobody acted. It has no save rate and no offer cost.
- **Expected retention profit** = Σ over contacted customers of [P(churn) × save rate × horizon × monthly bill − offer cost]. It measures what a campaign earns, and it depends on campaign assumptions ($100 offer, 30% save rate, 12-month horizon) that are not in the data.

On the test customers: $341K at risk, against a $29.9K realised retention profit. The app's Retention Strategy section shows both, live, as the assumptions change.
